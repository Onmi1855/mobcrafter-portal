Guidelines

Community and upload rules

These guidelines keep Hub releases usable, searchable, and safe to share without breaking the existing MobCrafter workflow.

Publish a release Terms

Rights and attribution

  • Only upload content you created or have permission to redistribute.
  • Do not upload copyrighted characters, textures, or models unless the license allows it.
  • Do not mislabel community work as official starter content.

Metadata quality

  • Use clear titles, honest descriptions, and relevant tags.
  • List dependency mods when your build uses blocks or assets outside vanilla or MobCrafter itself.
  • Mark external or supporter-only access truthfully so users know what to expect before clicking.

Safety and moderation

  • Do not upload malware, misleading archives, or broken files disguised as working releases.
  • Do not use thumbnails or descriptions that impersonate another creator or hide the actual contents of the release.
  • Moderators may reject, unpublish, or remove releases that violate these rules.
