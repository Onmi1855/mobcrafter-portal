Contact and support routes

The Hub currently exposes creator-level support links on release and creator pages. A centralized public operator inbox has not been published in this repository yet, so this page documents the current contact boundaries without inventing one.

Release-specific help

If a creator published a support URL or external release page, use that route for questions about files, install steps, supporter rewards, or creator-side updates.

Site-wide issues

For site-wide issues such as broken public metadata, moderation concerns, or future legal notices, this page will publish the public operator contact route once it exists. Until then, do not treat creator support URLs as a site-operator inbox.

Owner edits

If you are the creator of a release, use the creator dashboard and edit flow to correct metadata, links, or publication state on your own submissions.

This page intentionally avoids publishing guessed email addresses or unverified social links.
