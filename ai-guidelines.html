AI-assisted release guidelines

Hub allows AI-assisted workflows, but the result must still be reviewed by a human creator and disclosed honestly in the release metadata.

Open Quick Upload Community guidelines

Required disclosure

If AI helped generate the build, textures, workflow, or release packaging, mark the creation method accordingly and use the note field to explain the role AI played.

Human review is mandatory

  • Check block placement, dependencies, links, and archive contents before publishing.
  • Do not publish unreviewed AI output as if it were verified gameplay-ready content.
  • Do not claim official starter status for AI output unless it is actually curated and flagged by moderation.

Rights still matter

AI use does not remove copyright or license obligations. If the output includes material you do not have the right to share, do not upload it.
