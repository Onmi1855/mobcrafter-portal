Copyright complaint guidance

This page explains what a rights complaint should contain. It does not invent a legal inbox that is not yet published in the repository.

What to include

  • Identification of the copyrighted work you believe is infringed.
  • The exact Hub URL or URLs involved.
  • Your contact information and authority to act for the rightsholder.
  • A good-faith statement and a statement made under penalty of perjury.
  • Your physical or electronic signature.

Important boundary

Creator support URLs and external release pages are not legal notice channels for the Hub itself. A dedicated legal contact route will be published here when it is available.

What happens next

Moderation may unpublish or remove disputed content while a complaint is reviewed. Repeat or obvious infringement can also affect future publication status.
